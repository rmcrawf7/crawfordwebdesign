FAQ

Good to know before we start.

Who owns my website and domain?

Your domain should be registered in your name or your business account. Access details are covered in the project agreement.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, if it's built on an editable platform like WordPress or Shopify. Custom-coded sites may need code changes, so we can set up an update arrangement instead.

Are hosting and domain fees included?

Not automatically. I'll explain any recurring costs before launch so there are no surprises.

How do payments work?

50% deposit before work begins and 50% at completion. Interac e-Transfer is accepted, and terms are confirmed in writing.

How long does a website take?

It depends on scope, revisions and how quickly content comes together. You'll get an estimated schedule with your quote.
