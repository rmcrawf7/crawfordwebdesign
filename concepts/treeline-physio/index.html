FAQ

Before your first visit.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly with us. Some insurance plans ask for a referral, so it's worth checking your policy.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the area we're treating, like shorts for a knee injury.

How long is an appointment?

Initial assessments are about 45 minutes. Follow-up visits are usually 30 minutes.

Do you have parking?

Yes, free parking is available right outside the clinic.
