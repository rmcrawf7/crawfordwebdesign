Common questions

Good to know before we start

Who owns my website and domain?

You should register your domain in your own name or business account. Website ownership and access details will be documented in your project agreement.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes, if we choose an editable platform such as WordPress or Shopify. A custom static website may require code changes or an ongoing update arrangement.

Are hosting and domain fees included?

Not automatically. We recommend a hosting solution that fits your website and budget, and explain recurring costs before launch.

How do payments work?

After we agree on the scope and quote, we send an invoice. For most website projects, payment is split into a 50% deposit before work begins and a 50% final payment at the agreed completion milestone. Interac e-Transfer is available. Payment terms will be confirmed in writing before the project starts.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the project scope, revisions, and when content is supplied. You'll receive an estimated schedule with your quote.
